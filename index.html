Backup & calculation notes

Stored in this browser only. Protein, fat and carbohydrate energy estimates use modified Atwater factors (3.5 / 8.5 / 3.5 kcal/g). Minerals use mg/100 kcal. Consumed totals include meals marked Fed; projected totals include pending meals and exclude skipped meals. Blank consumed amounts assume the planned amount was fully consumed. Existing meals without status are treated as pending. Food entries are shared across cats; changing a food updates historical calculations. No automatic kidney-disease diagnosis or dietary targets. Guaranteed minimums are not exact nutrient measurements.
